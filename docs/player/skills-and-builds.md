# Skills and builds

A **build** is what a character brings to a fight: two professions, eight
skills and the attribute points behind them. Open one with **Edit** on
the main menu, or **New character**.

## Professions

There are ten: Warrior, Ranger, Monk, Necromancer, Mesmer,
Elementalist, Assassin, Ritualist, Paragon and Dervish. Your **primary**
profession is what you are: its skills, and its **primary attribute**
(marked ★), which only a primary gets. Your **secondary** lends you its
skills, but not its primary attribute.

## The skill bar

The bar has **eight slots**. Only one **elite** skill (gold frame) fits on
a bar: equip a second and it replaces the first. Press **×** on a slot to
empty it.

**Flash enchantments** (Dervish) are instant: using one keeps your
swing and your stride, so you can weave them between blows. Each one
turns your other flash enchantments off for 1 second.

**Forms** (the five Dervish Avatars: Balthazar, Dwayna, Grenth, Lyssa
and Melandru) change you for over a minute. A form is
not an enchantment, so nobody can strip it, and you can be in one form
at a time. Using one disables it for 45 seconds. When a form ends, you
stop what you were doing: your attack, your walk and any skill you were
activating. Press a movement key again to walk on.

## Attributes

You have **200 points** to spend. Each rank of an attribute costs more
than the last, up to rank 12; the row shows what the next one costs.
Twelve, twelve and three spends all of it. Runes and headgear can add up
to four more ranks on top; see [Equipment](equipment.md). Raising the
attribute a skill uses makes it stronger, and the numbers in its
description change as you spend.

## The library

The skill library lists every skill your two professions can use,
grouped by attribute. Search names and text, show only elites, or pick
skill types to narrow it. Skills of other professions wait, folded, under
**Other professions**.

**Show skills not in the game yet** adds the GW1 skills VibeWars does not
have yet, greyed out. You can read them, not equip them.

Beside the editor, the **Training Ground** lets you try your bar on
practice targets before a match.

## Sharing builds

In the editor's **⋯** menu, **Share** gives your build as a line of
text, and **Share as GW1 code** as a GW1 template code. Press **Copy**
and send it.

To take one in, paste it anywhere in the editor that is not a text
field. Both kinds work. A GW1 skill VibeWars does not have yet arrives as
an empty slot. The build is yours once you press **Save**.

A team code is pasted on **Team Play** instead.

## Team Builds

A **team build** is a team of its own characters, made on **Team Play**
and kept apart from your Solo characters. Each one is a card there:
**New team build** makes one, **Edit** opens it, and a click on a card
picks it for your next Team Play lobby. Add a member
with **New character**, or **Add** one of your characters to copy its
build in. A copy is the team's own: changing it, or the character it came
from, leaves the other as it was. Press a member's name to edit it in the
build editor; its **Save** puts it back in the team, and the team's own
**Save** keeps it all.
