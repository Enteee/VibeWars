# Controls

Every key in the table below can be changed in **Options**, under
**Controls**; Esc, Enter, F10 and the mouse buttons are fixed. Pick a
**Preset** there, or click a key cell and press the key you want; a key
already in use moves to the new action, and the screen says which one
lost it. Changes save at once.

## Keys

The default preset is **VibeWars**. **Classic** is for GW1 hands.

| Action                     | VibeWars         | Classic                |
| -------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Skills 1 to 8              | Q W E R, A S D F | 1 to 8                 |
| Move                       | arrow keys       | W A S D, or arrow keys |
| Weapon sets 1 to 4         | 1 to 4           | F1 to F4               |
| Attack, or use a flag      | Space            | Space                  |
| Next foe (Shift: previous) | Tab              | Tab                    |
| Closest foe                | C                | C                      |
| Your team's called foe     | T                | T                      |
| Yourself                   | G                | G                      |
| Show names (hold)          | Shift            | Shift                  |
| Mute                       | M                | M                      |
| Drop flag, relic or ashes  | none             | none                   |

These keys are fixed:

- **Esc** cancels what you are doing: a cast, an attack, a walk. Pressed
  again, it clears your target. It also closes Help and Options.
- **Enter** opens the chat, or click the chat icon at the bottom left;
  Enter sends, and Esc closes it. Start a line with `!` to talk to
  everyone, or `#` for your team only. Closed, the chat shows only its
  newest lines, which fade after a few seconds, and clicks go through it.
- **F10** asks whether to leave the match.

## Mouse

- **Left click** the ground to walk there. Without Shift, the left button only walks.
- On maps with raised platforms, a left click goes to the surface you see, and **Alt + left click** to the ground below it. A ring under the cursor marks a platform, a dashed ring the ground.
- **Right click** a foe or an ally to target it. Right click the same foe
  again, quickly, to attack it.
- **Right click** a flag or the flag stand, in a Guild Battle or on Barrow Field, to target
  it; **Space** walks there and takes the flag up or plants it.
- **Right click** a lever to target it; **Space** walks there and flips it, opening or closing the gates it works.
- Walk into a **portal** and it moves you to its exit. A lever may switch it on or off; after a trip it rests 5 s before it takes you again.
- A **shimmering gate** stops bodies, arrows and other projectiles; a spell cast straight at a target passes. A **cracked wall** can be broken: right-click it, then attack it or hit it with a damage skill.
- **Wheel click** a foe to call it for your team: their **T** targets it.
  On the ground or an ally, it pings that spot. Or click the target bar
  at the top to call the foe you have targeted.
- Click the compass to ping that spot for your team; drag on it to draw a
  line they see for a few seconds.
- **Wheel** zooms. **Right drag** looks around; let go and the view
  glides back to you.
- Click a skill on the bar to use it, and a name in the party window to
  target that ally.
- Hold **Show names** (Shift) and every name shows, each player's with
  their signet before it: a small shield that is always the same for the
  same player. While held, clicking a character's name works as clicking
  its body does. A right click only targets, even twice; a left click
  targets and attacks at once; a wheel click calls or pings. The name a
  click would hit is highlighted.

## The top bar

Outside a match, the top bar holds **[?]** (this Help), **⚙** (Options),
**Lobby** (your invite link, Join lobby, Open/Closed, Leave, and who is in your lobby; its badge counts them),
**♪** (music: what plays, and how loud), the sound mute and fullscreen. In a match the same buttons,
but Lobby, sit at the top right of the screen. In a narrow window, these icons and
the screen's other buttons fold into **⋯**; only its main button stays out.
⚙ opens Options as a window under the bar, the screen still behind it; Esc, × or a click outside closes it. Under **Display**, **Lighting** turns a lit map's light and shade on or off, at once, even in a match.
Help closes with the × at its top right, Esc, or another press of [?].

**Share a beat with your lobby.** In the beat editor (♪, then **Edit**),
**Share with lobby** sends what you have typed, saved or not, to everyone
else in a lobby you hosted or joined by link (never one the queue found;
at most 8 KB, one share every ten seconds). They see a badge on ♪, and
under **Shared with you** in its window a row per player, "Ann · Ember Deep",
with **Play**. Nothing plays until you press Play, only outside a match;
**Back to my music** returns to yours. A shared beat is never saved: it is
gone when you leave the lobby or reload.

In Auto, the menus share one song that builds as you go deeper, and each map always has its own song.
