# Canyon pilot study (#3070)

Study: `3070-canyon.png` (800x720: plan at 0.286 of 2800x1800, cross-section, palette).
Regenerate with `node scripts/assets/canyon-study.mjs docs/art` (needs inkscape; the SVG
is not committed). Layout is new and asymmetric; the PNG is a guide, not coordinates.

- **Place:** a winding dry river bed (L0, `#6d5a47`) cut between rust sandstone rims
  (L2 top `#9a7a62`, lip `#c9ad94`, cliff face `#5e4538`). Desaturated brown, never
  danger orange; the only warm saturation is torch light.
- **Heights:** ledges on the north cliff (L1), a stone arch over the bed at x=250
  (walk over, pass under), a ruined watchtower on the north rim (L2, holes and rubble), and west of it a watch
  house (#3179) whose flat roof (L3, sunlit, a lantern) is reached by stairs between
  the two and joined to the tower top by a plank walk,
  a terraced path of three plaster steps on the south rim down to L1, a drop-off from
  the south lip into the bed.
- **Elements:** rope bridge at the narrowest point (x=400) with a brass lever on its
  north end; cave under the south rim with two mouths (bed side x=470 and x=700) and
  a torch at each (`#ffb060`, radius 150); scorched patches in the west half; a mire
  where the river seeps in the east.
- **Light:** sun from the NW (directionDeg 315), low (dusk row of visual-style §2):
  hard SE shadows, north cliff shades the bed.
- **Teams:** A west, B east on the bed; sides differ on purpose (A has the arch and
  ledges, B the mire, cave mouth and terraces) and must be balanced by the bench and
  high-ground numbers, not by mirroring.
- **Outline:** not a rectangle: a ragged rim edge, outside is natural land.
- **Layers since #3179:** the map is four layers, so the bed moved up to make room
  for tunnels: tunnels 0, bed 1 (the ground), rims 2, tower and house tops 3. The
  study's L1 ledges are now rim-height shelves reached from the bed by their ramps,
  and the arch stands at rim height. Under the bed, a tunnel network: two stair
  shafts a side, torch-lit galleries, and a junction chamber west of the centre with
  its own shaft and a brazier. The south-west shaft comes up at the foot of the ramp
  to the south-west shelf.
