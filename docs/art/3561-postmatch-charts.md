# Post-match charts: option A (#3561, #3565)

The look the operator chose for the post-match charts on the queue
screen. `3561-postmatch-a-you.png` and `3561-postmatch-a-teams.png` are
the mockups, invented data in the UI's own palette; the `.svg` files are
their sources.

- Scores on the left, the chart panel on the right, tabs **You | Teams**.
  The mockups also show a **By skill** tab: it is out of scope and not
  built.
- One event strip under the time axis. Crowded marks go up one row. The
  legend chips sit under the strip.
- Under 1000 px the charts go under the scores. On a phone the scores
  collapse to you and two more, with **Show all**; Queue again and Back to menu sit
  in a footer clear of the charts.
- Series differ by dash as well as hue: dealt solid, taken dashed,
  healing dotted, energy dash-dot. Teams wear the game's team colours.
- Event shapes: kill diamond, death cross, interrupt bolt, objective
  flag, resurrection triangle.
