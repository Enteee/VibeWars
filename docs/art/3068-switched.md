# Switched structures (#3068)

Study: `3068-switched.png` (800x510), regenerate with
`node scripts/assets/switched-study.mjs docs/art` (needs inkscape; the SVG is not
committed).

- **Drawbridge**: down it is a plank deck (`#a07a48`) with rope rails and a stone
  hinge sill, spanning water. Up it stands as an iron-banded oak slab (`#7a4e26`) at
  the hinge, chains taut: a vertical slab and an open channel read as "no crossing"
  without colour.
- **Rope bridge**: whole is the plank-bridge look. Cut, two halves hang from their
  anchors with swinging planks and a brass fray mark; nothing spans the gap.
- **Collapsing floor**: a ruin deck. Cracks are dark `#1c2330` 2.2 px lines: none, a
  few, then wide cracks with chipped-out black holes with a lit lip. Fallen is a dark
  pit with rubble and broken deck stubs at the corners. The stage is told by crack
  count and holes, not colour.
- **Portcullis**: a stone arch, an iron grid (`#7a8aa4`) with spiked tips. Open, the
  grid sits raised above the lintel and the opening is dark.
- **Secret door**: shut is the rock wall, with no seam and no tell (the study cell is
  the wall alone, the same art as the portcullis' wall). Open, a dark opening with a
  lit top lip and a shaded jamb.
