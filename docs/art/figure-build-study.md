# Figure build study (#4176)

[`figure-build-study.png`](figure-build-study.png) (the `.svg` beside it is the same drawing), generated by `scripts/assets/figure-build-study.mjs`. It shows the five builds on the warrior, monk and ranger outfits of [the outfit study](profession-outfit-study.png): ally east, foe west, the four camera zooms, and the menu figure (monk, facing the viewer). Team colour, badge, cape and the flat weapon marks are not touched.

A build is `BUILD_SHAPES[build] = { shoulder, waist, hip, hair }`: it scales a slice's width by region (legs and skirt by `hip`, the torso by the mean of `waist` and `shoulder`, arms, pauldrons and mantles by `shoulder`) and adds a hair cue behind the head.

| build       | shoulder | waist | hip  | hair cue                                               |
| ----------- | -------- | ----- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| male        | 1.25     | 1.00  | 0.85 | none                                                   |
| female      | 0.78     | 0.80  | 1.20 | braid: 7 slices trailing behind and down from the head |
| nonbinary A | 1.00     | 0.92  | 1.00 | a side fringe over the brow, one slice                 |
| nonbinary B | 0.88     | 0.84  | 1.10 | a bun at the back of the head, one slice               |
| nonbinary C | 1.15     | 0.82  | 0.92 | a short tail, 4 slices                                 |

Reading notes: at the two smaller zooms only the shoulder width and the hair survive, so the hair cue carries the read there; the male and female builds differ most, then the three nonbinary options differ from them by the hair cue and a middle build. Option A is the most neutral (the widths stay near 1, so it changes the outfit least); B and C each lean one way, B toward the female build, C toward the male one.

Recommendation: **A**, because a nonbinary figure that is a blend of the other two reads as a mix; a distinct neutral reads as its own. That is the asset-designer's view only. **The operator's pick is recorded here once made:** _pending_.
