# Lifts (#3079)

Study: `3079-lift.png` (800x340), regenerate with
`node scripts/assets/lift-study.mjs docs/art` (needs inkscape; the SVG is not
committed).

- **Frame**: an iron frame (`#7a8aa4`) with a crossbeam around a dark shaft, between
  the upper deck (left) and the low floor (right). The platform is a plank deck
  (`#a07a48`, dark underside) hung on two brass chains from the beam, drawn at its
  current height: flush with the low floor at rest low, flush with the upper deck at
  rest high, in between while moving. Chain length shows the height.
- **Rest vs travel is told by the ends, not by colour**: at rest the sides are open,
  and the platform meets a floor. Travelling, an iron bar gate stands at each end and
  a brass chevron pair on the shaft points the way (up or down). Nobody gets on or
  off while the bars are up.
- **Carried bodies** are drawn standing on the platform at its current height,
  so they rise and sink with it.
- **Timer lift**: a clock and a rest / travel strip (4 s, 2 s, 4 s, 2 s; travel
  segments are taller and brass). **Lever lift**: a lever, handle at one side, then
  the other, each flip sends the lift to the other level. The same lever art as the
  other levers.
- **Plan view** (for the in-world top-down read): a wood square in a dark shaft; while
  travelling, dashed iron bars close both ends.
