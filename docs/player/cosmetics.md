# Cosmetics

Cosmetics change how your name, the ground under you, your swing and your cape look to other players. They are purely for looks: no cosmetic changes how anything plays, and none ever covers a cue you read in a fight. They belong to your player key, so all your characters share them.

## Earning them

You earn cosmetics with verified ranked wins, as the public leaderboard counts them.
Nobody hands them out: anyone can check the leaderboard and see what you earned.

them again. What you picked stays picked.

## On the ground

A ground aura is a faint ring at your feet. A footstep trail leaves small motes where you walk, which fade within a moment. Both lie under every ring, mark and telegraph, and never use a team colour. With reduced motion turned on in your system, the aura holds still and no trail is drawn.

## On your weapon

A weapon trail is a ribbon behind your weapon as a melee attack strikes, fading within a fifth of a second of the hit. Bows, spears, wands and staves get none, and no spell or cast ever shows one. With reduced motion turned on in your system, no weapon trail is drawn.

## Coming back and falling

A spawn-in style gathers small motes at your feet as you spawn or are resurrected. A death dissolve scatters them as you fall. Each takes exactly as long as the plain return and fall, lies under your body, and leaves your outline, team ring and corpse as easy to read as ever. With reduced motion turned on in your system, only the plain fade is drawn.

## On your cape

An emblem frame is a thin border round the emblem on your guild cape. It never covers, moves or changes the emblem, and is drawn in your cape's own trim colour or a plain metal, never a team colour. You need a guild cape to wear one: the slot shows in Options once you wear a guild cape.

## Wearing them

Open Options and pick **Cosmetics**. Each slot is one row of swatches, each a
small picture of its item; the first, an empty circle struck through, wears
nothing. Your pick has a blue ring. The line beside the row (under it on a
phone) names the swatch you point at or focus, and what earns it. A locked
swatch is dimmed, with a small padlock. Your choice applies at once. Other
players see it from the next lobby you join.

Beside the rows (above them on a phone), your character wears your look and
wanders about, walking, standing, swinging and casting, so the aura, trail and
weapon trail show in motion. It stays in view while you scroll. The Primary and
Secondary pickers under it show the look on any profession pair; they change
only the preview, never your character. Press and hold a swatch to
try it on, even a locked one: the line says what you are previewing and, while
it is locked, what earns it. Let go on an earned item to wear it. Let go on a
locked one, or slide off the swatch, and nothing changes. With a keyboard, hold
Space on a swatch.

## Seeing other players' cosmetics

Other players see your cosmetics only if their copy of the leaderboard shows you
earned them.
