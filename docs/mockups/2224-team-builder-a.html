Idea: a row opens in place like an accordion: professions, attribute list with − / + and points left, gear summary with weapon sets, all in one block under the row.

Fixes: everything about a member is visible and editable at once; no popover hunting; the skills stay on screen above the edit.

Costs: the expanded row pushes the rest down (the list scrolls), and one block is a lot of UI to open for a one-point change.
