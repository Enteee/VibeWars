#2140 C: mods in the library

The library's gear tab gets Prefixes, Suffixes and Inscriptions groups of mod cards, like skills. Click a ◇ socket on a weapon to select it (the library filters to what fits it), then click a card, or drag a card onto a socket, as skills go onto the bar. The attribute is pill toggles on the weapon slot. To take a mod off: × on its socket, the None card, or drag the chip back to the library (as worn gear is today).

Idea: mods are collectibles like skills: browse them in the library, put them on a socket the way a skill goes on the bar. Fixes: no native selects; one place to browse every mod (unfiltered, it doubles as a mod reference); drag and click both work. Costs: two places to look (socket in the centre, cards on the right); the gear tab gets long; with no socket selected a card's target is ambiguous, so a click needs a selected socket first; the slots grow as in B.
