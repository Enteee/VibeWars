# Cosmetics

Cosmetics change how your name, and the ground under you, look to other players. They are purely for looks: no cosmetic changes how anything plays, and none ever covers a cue you read in a fight. They belong to your player key, so all your characters share them.

## Earning them

You earn cosmetics with verified ranked wins, as the public leaderboard counts them.
Nobody hands them out: anyone can check the leaderboard and see what you earned.

| Item                                | Earned by              |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Jade frame and the Contender title  | 5 ranked wins          |
| Moss aura and Moss trail            | 5 ranked wins          |
| Rose frame and the Veteran title    | 25 ranked wins         |
| Ember aura and Ember trail          | 25 ranked wins         |
| Frost aura and Frost trail          | 75 ranked wins         |
| Teal frame and the Warlord title    | 200 ranked wins        |
| Starlight aura and Starlight trail  | 500 ranked wins        |
| Orchid frame and the Champion title | A place in the Top 100 |
| Win-streak flame                    | 3 ranked wins in a row |

The Top 100 items and the flame last only while you hold them. Drop out of the Top
100, or lose a ranked match, and they go back to the default look until you earn
them again. What you picked stays picked.

## On the ground

A ground aura is a faint ring at your feet. A footstep trail leaves small motes where you walk, which fade within a moment. Both lie under every ring, mark and telegraph, and never use a team colour. With reduced motion turned on in your system, the aura holds still and no trail is drawn.

## Wearing them

Open Options and pick **Cosmetics**. Each slot lists its items, each marked as
wearable or locked, with what earns it. Your choice applies at once. Other players
see it from the next lobby you join.

## Seeing other players' cosmetics

Other players see your cosmetics only if their copy of the leaderboard shows you
earned them. To see everyone in the default look, turn off **Show other players'
cosmetics** in Options under **Cosmetics**.
