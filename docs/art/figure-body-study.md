# Figure body study (#4176)

[`figure-body-study.png`](figure-body-study.png) (the `.svg` beside it is the same drawing), generated by `scripts/assets/figure-body-study.mjs`. Three independent axes, three options each, 27 combinations: **height**, **build** and **hair**. One panel per axis (the other two held at average / cropped), each on the warrior, monk and ranger outfits of [the outfit study](profession-outfit-study.png): ally east, foe west, the four camera zooms, and the menu figure (monk, facing the viewer). A strip of five mixed combinations closes the sheet. Team colour, badge, cape and the flat weapon marks are not touched. No gender labels anywhere.

## Numbers proposed

**Height** scales the lift of every slice (the stack's vertical extent), not its width.

| height  | scale |
| ------- | ----- |
| short   | 0.92  |
| average | 1.00  |
| tall    | 1.08  |

**Build**: `BUILD_SHAPES[build] = { shoulder, chest, waist, limb, lean }`. A slice's width is scaled by region (arms, pauldrons and mantles by `shoulder`; the upper torso by `chest`; hem, skirt and robe by `waist`; legs by `limb`, which also widens the stance). `lean` shifts the upper body forward along the heading, in body radii: torso 0.6x, arms 0.8x, shoulders 1x, head 1.3x of it.

| build   | shoulder | chest | waist | limb | lean | posture                           |
| ------- | -------- | ----- | ----- | ---- | ---- | --------------------------------- |
| lean    | 0.84     | 0.80  | 0.76  | 0.82 | 0.00 | narrow, upright                   |
| average | 1.00     | 1.00  | 1.00  | 1.00 | 0.04 | as the outfit study               |
| heavy   | 1.24     | 1.22  | 1.24  | 1.20 | 0.12 | broad, shoulders and head forward |

**Hair**: neutral dark `#3a2e26`, never team colour, drawn behind or below the headgear.

| hair    | slices                                                                 |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| cropped | one small slice at the nape: barely visible, the "no hair cue" option  |
| long    | six slices falling behind the head to the shoulders, 0.30 to 0.21 wide |
| tied    | a tie at the back of the head and a tail of five slices trailing back  |

## Reading notes

At the two smaller zooms only height, shoulder width and the hair silhouette survive, so heavy and tall read best; lean vs average is the subtlest pair there and reads mostly on the menu figure and the first zoom. Long and tied are distinct at every zoom, because the tail points away from the body; cropped is by design the quiet one. Tall + heavy stays inside the 64 px figure box on all three outfits in the sheet (warrior's winged-less helm is the tallest).
