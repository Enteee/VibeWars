# Bots

A **bot** is a player the game plays for you. In a lobby you host, bots
play every place nobody takes, so you can practise, or play with fewer
friends.

## Who plays a bot

Nobody adds bots: a lobby lists its people only. When the
[Team Lobby](modes.md#team-lobby) ends, each side plays the Team Build it
chose, and every one of its characters nobody claimed is played by a bot,
under that character's name. A side with nobody on it plays a random
preset team, all bots. Someone who leaves during the Team Lobby leaves their
character to a bot.

In the lobby and the Team Lobby a bot looks like anyone else. In the
match it is tagged **BOT** on its nameplate, the target frame, the party
window and the match summary.

Bots are made for one match: once it is over, the lobby lists its people
again, and the next match's bots are made afresh.

## How bots fight

A bot plays the role its build gives it. One wielding a melee weapon is
**frontline**: it closes in and attacks its target. One with two or more
heals or protections it can cast on an ally is a **healer**: it stands
behind whichever ally is hurt most, away from the foes. Every other bot is
**ranged**: it keeps where every one of its attacks reaches, and stands
and fights a foe who comes too close. A team turns on any foe in reach
who drops below half health, to finish them.

## Random Arena

A Random Arena has no bots: every place is a real player, and you wait
until enough are queued.
