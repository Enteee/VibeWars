# Creature stack study (#4171–#4173)

Study for the three creature cards of epic #3974: every creature look as stacked slices in the ADR-0057 body style (1 px `#08080a` outline, dark-to-light slices, shadow, team plate under the figure and never baked). Image: [`creature-stack-study.png`](creature-stack-study.png), 337 KB. It is generated by `scripts/assets/creature-stack-study.mjs`, whose builders use `sprite-stack.ts`'s units (body radii, `x` forward, `y` right, `STACK_STEP` 1/12 per lift), so a developer ports them one to one. The slice counts and tones below are what the script draws.

## Reading at match zoom

- **Silhouette first.** At 1×, the spirit is a green cone, the flesh golem is the widest and tallest mass, the beast and the rat are low and long (the only horizontal silhouettes), the horrors are tall bone pillars with a pale skull, and the minion is the same pillar at 0.7. Horrors differ by a 4–6 px cue: a spike (fiend), a red glow (vampiric), a lean (shambling), side spurs (jagged). The look icon stays the exact identifier.
- **Guard against a player body.** The guard is steel grey with no profession glyph, a spear above the head and a round shield, where a body is team-coloured with a glyph. The plate carries team colour for both.
- **Skeletons are dark.** The rib gap ring dominates, so a skeleton reads as a dark pillar with pale rib arcs and a pale skull. The skull is the readable part: keep it lit and forward. If the dev wants more bone at 1×, lighten the gap tone (`#2a2622` → `#4a443c`) before adding slices.
- **Turning.** The 8-heading rows show the horror and guard turn clearly: the skull and the spear move round the stack. Quadrupeds read their heading from the head and tail.
- **Walk.** Stride moves shins and fists by ±0.3 body radii; for the beast, diagonal leg pairs swap.

## Atlas cost

Frames follow the radius, so small looks bake small. A rough budget, 4 B a pixel at about 3 px a radius unit squared plus the lifts: the spirit one frame; the rat about 16 × 20 px × 24 frames; the golem the largest, about 64 × 90 px × 24 frames, 550 KB. All 14 looks stay inside the 2 MB creature allowance if frames are tight-cropped, not square, which the card 1 test should check by measuring.
