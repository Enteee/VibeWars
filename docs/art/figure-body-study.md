## Numbers proposed

**Height** scales the lift of every slice (the stack's vertical extent), not its width.

| height  | scale |
| ------- | ----- |
| short   | 0.92  |
| average | 1.00  |
| tall    | 1.08  |

**Build**: `BUILD_SHAPES[build] = { shoulder, chest, waist, limb, lean }`. A slice's width is scaled by region (arms, pauldrons and mantles by `shoulder`; the upper torso by `chest`; hem, skirt and robe by `waist`; legs by `limb`, which also widens the stance). `lean` shifts the upper body forward along the heading, in body radii: torso 0.6x, arms 0.8x, shoulders 1x, head 1.3x of it.

| build   | shoulder | chest | waist | limb | lean | posture                           |
| ------- | -------- | ----- | ----- | ---- | ---- | --------------------------------- |

| heavy   | 1.24     | 1.22  | 1.24  | 1.20 | 0.12 | broad, shoulders and head forward |
