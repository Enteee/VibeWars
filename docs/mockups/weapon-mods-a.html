Idea: one gear UI: armor and weapons are both picked where they sit on the body, from the same popover. Fixes: no more native selects (None at the top of each list removes); every mod shows its effect; the attribute is visible and one click away; the slots shrink back to what is held. Costs: the popover is wide (three lists, up to 21 rows) and covers the library while open; seven hotspots on a small figure; the off-hand's ⑦ disappears with a two-handed weapon, so the numbers shift meaning per set.
