# Skills and builds

A **build** is what a character brings to a fight: two professions, eight
skills and the attribute points behind them. Open one with **Edit** on
the main menu, or **New character**.

## Professions

There are ten: Warrior, Ranger, Monk, Necromancer, Mesmer,
Elementalist, Assassin, Ritualist, Paragon and Dervish. Your **primary**
profession is what you are: its skills, and its **primary attribute**
(marked ★), which only a primary gets. Your **secondary** lends you its
skills, but not its primary attribute.

## The skill bar

The bar has **eight slots**. Only one **elite** skill (gold frame) fits on
a bar: equip a second and it replaces the first. Press **×** on a slot to
empty it.

**Flash enchantments** (Dervish) are instant: using one keeps your
swing and your stride, so you can weave them between blows. Each one
turns your other flash enchantments off for 1 second.

**Forms** (the five Dervish Avatars: Balthazar, Dwayna, Grenth, Lyssa
and Melandru) change you for over a minute. A form is
not an enchantment, so nobody can strip it, and you can be in one form
at a time. Using one disables it for 45 seconds. When a form ends, you
stop what you were doing: your attack, your walk and any skill you were
activating. Press a movement key again to walk on.

**Some shots disable your other attacks.** Focused Shot and Needling
Shot fly faster than an arrow should, but each one that hits turns your
other attack skills off for a few seconds. Spells are unaffected.

## Attributes

You have **200 points** to spend. Each rank of an attribute costs more
than the last, up to rank 12; the row shows what the next one costs.
Twelve, twelve and three spends all of it. Runes and headgear can add up
to four more ranks on top; see [Equipment](equipment.md). Raising the
attribute a skill uses makes it stronger: numbers that grow with the
attribute are shown in green and bold, at your current ranks including
runes and headgear.

In a fight your ranks can change for a while. Weakness takes one off
each. Some hexes set a foe's attributes to 0, and Master of Magic sets
your elemental ones to 13: a rank set this way overrides runes and
Weakness alike. Signet of Illusions lets your next spells of other
attributes use your Illusion Magic rank. Some skills raise your
attributes for a time or for your next spells, as Glyph of Elemental
Power and Ritual Lord do, even one you left at 0; a set rank, as Master
of Magic's, beats such a boost.

## Echoes

A Paragon's **echoes** are long buffs on one ally. A **refrain** renews
to its full length, and a **finale** takes effect, whenever a chant or a
shout ends on that ally. A chant or shout reapplied before it ends has not
ended. Nothing removes an echo: it ends when it runs out or its bearer
dies. Most cannot target a spirit.

## Skills that become another skill

Some Mesmer skills put another skill in their own slot for a while.
Arcane Echo and Echo copy the next skill you use. **Inspired
Enchantment** and **Inspired Hex** become the skill that cast the
enchantment or hex they removed, for 20 seconds. **Arcane Thievery**
becomes the spell it disabled on your foe, for as long as the disable
lasts. The copy has its own recharge. When it ends, the original skill
comes back and starts its own recharge, and a cast of the copy still in
progress fails. A status put on by a spirit or by another status cannot
be copied, though removing it still gives you the Energy.

## Wells and glyphs

A Necromancer's **wells** exploit the nearest corpse. With none near, a
well sacrifices 66% of your Health instead and appears where you stand.

An Elementalist's **glyphs** change your next spell: it cannot be
interrupted, casts instantly, recharges at once, or costs less. A new
glyph replaces the one you had.

## Interventions

A skill that answers "the next time damage drops Health below N%"
answers any blow or life steal that leaves you alive and below the
line, even when you were already below it before the hit, and never
health you sacrifice or lose to degeneration. Fatal-damage protection, as Judge's Intervention,
drops the whole killing blow: no damage, no death. Faithful Intervention
and Illusion of Weakness have no duration: they last until damage, a
strip or death ends them, and heal you as they go.

## The library

The skill library lists every skill your two professions can use,
grouped by attribute. Search names and text, show only elites, or pick
skill types to narrow it. Skills of other professions wait, folded, under
**Other professions**.

**Show skills not in the game yet** adds the GW1 skills VibeWars does not
have yet, greyed out. You can read them, not equip them.

Beside the editor, the **Training Ground** lets you try your bar on
practice targets before a match. Its log shows every skill you or a bot
use, the adrenaline you gain, and a skill that had no effect, with what
it was waiting for. Some targets move: in **Moving targets** one walks a
loop, one flees from you and one chases you.

## Item spells

A Ritualist's **item spells** put a spirit's **ashes** in your hands: a
bonus while you hold them, and a **drop effect** when they fall. They
drop when they run out, when you die, when you press **Drop ashes** above
your Health bar, cast another item spell, shadow step, swap weapons or
take up a flag or relic. Your hands are full while you hold them: no
attacks and no attack skills, but you walk at full speed and still cast
spells. Nothing removes ashes; every end is a drop.

## Shadow steps

A **shadow step** moves you at once, no walk in between. Most step you to
your target; some take you back to where you cast them when they end, as
Shadow of Haste and Shadow Walk do. **Recall** steps you to its ally only
when it ends, and disables all your skills for 3 seconds. **Swap** trades
places with one of your side's spirits or minions.

## Sharing builds

In the editor's **⋯** menu, **Share** gives your build as a line of
text, and **Share as GW1 code** as a GW1 template code. Press **Copy**
and send it.

To take one in, choose **Load GW1 code** under **⋯**, paste the code
and press **Load**. If it cannot load, it says why under the field. As a
shortcut, you can also paste a code anywhere in the editor that is not a
text field. Both kinds of code work either way. A GW1 skill VibeWars
does not have yet arrives as an empty slot. The build is yours once you
press **Save**.

**Save** keeps your build and takes you back to where you came from.
**Esc** leaves without saving, and asks first if you changed anything.
**Share**, **Load GW1 code**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** are under **⋯**.

A team code is pasted on **Team Builder** instead.

## Team Builds

A **team build** is a team of its own characters, made on **Team
Builder** and kept apart from your own characters. Each one is a card
there and on each team mode: **New team build** makes one, and a click
on a card, or its **Edit**, opens it. You propose one in the
[Team Lobby](modes.md#team-lobby). Add a member
with **New character**, or **Add** one of your characters to copy its
build in. A copy is the team's own: changing it, or the character it came
from, leaves the other as it was. Press a member's name to edit it in the
build editor; its **Save** puts it back in the team, and the team's own
**Save** keeps it all and takes you back to Team Builder. **Esc** leaves
without saving, and asks first. The team's **Share**, **Duplicate** and
**Delete** are under **⋯**.
