# Modes

Every arena names its mode in its `map.json`, as GW1's Random Arenas
do (#1185). A match plays the mode of its map, in a coded lobby and in
a Random Arena alike; nobody picks one. The lobby shows it under the
map name, and the match clock counts down to its time limit. What
death costs and how a match is won are in
[Death and scoring](combat.md#death-and-scoring).

## Annihilation

Defeat every foe. The dead stay down until resurrected and pay the
death penalty. A team wiped loses; at 8 minutes the team with the
highest total morale wins, and equal morale is lost by both. Every
arena but Pillar Hall plays it.

## Kill Count

GW1's The Crag: the most kills in 3 minutes. The dead respawn after
8 seconds and there is no death penalty. The first team to 15 kills
wins at once; at 3 minutes the team with more kills wins, and equal
kills is a draw. Pillar Hall plays it.
